## Things to expect (from the ROM, to confirm in Phase 5)

- HUD split by SPRITE 0 HIT, not a scanline IRQ (MMC1 has no IRQ). The ROM polls
  $2002 bit 6 (wait for clear, then wait for set) at three places:
  bank 4 $8250, bank 7 $F198 and bank 7 $F5A2. Likely gameplay HUD at the TOP
  (like NG2's split at line 48) plus the cinema scenes.
- Only 7 writes to $2005 in the first 128 KB scan: a small scroll routine, easy to
  follow in scroll.log.
- CHR ROM banked 4 KB at a time: cinema scenes may switch CHR mid-frame (NG2's
  cinema scenes did; left them untouched there).
- Flicker: the original game flickers sprites when more than 8 share a line (seen even
  on a CRT). Fixed by "No sprite flicker" (Phase 6 #2). Check whether it rotates or
  reverses OAM (NG2 rotated every frame).
- Uneven scrolling / enemy steps: NG2 had the camera on 1,2,1,2 and soldiers on 0/1 px
  steps. Expect the same family of code here; NG2's eased camera, bands kept 30 frames
  and eased glide are the fixes to start from (Contra's extras include them).

## Phase 5: the whole game (recorded play, 131,452 frames)

- Camera: horizontal only, NO vertical scrolling anywhere (playfield Y always 64).
  Steps 1,2,1,2 (1.5 px/frame) mostly, sometimes 1,1,1; back -1/-2. X spans 512.
- Split: only the HUD at line 56 (sprite 0 = slot 0, tile 00, Y 0x26, never rotated).
  No parallax layers. Non-scrolling rooms (bosses) and rests at X 0 merge playfield and
  HUD for seconds: 56 returns after > 30 frames. Fix: the line-56 split is kept for good
  once seen (HUD_SPLIT_LINE in bands_frame()).
- Sprites 8x16. OAM rotates every frame (slots 1-63; slot 0 fixed). Busiest line up to
  25 (bosses/fire), commonly 3-10: lots of NES flicker -> No sprite flicker matters.
- Movers (world coordinates): steps -3..3 px; most move every frame, some every other
  frame (30 Hz). Eased glide (NG2) applies.
- No every-other-frame blinkers (<= 4%, the hit blink): Steady shots not needed (harmless).
- Flashes: none in gameplay. The background goes black for a moment in cutscenes only;
  the 5-3 lightning is drawn graphics, not a palette flash. Nothing to soften.

## First play test at 240 (2026-11-15)

- Bug (mine): prepare() ran the self-check, which marks the frame unprepared, so present()
  gave up on every picture: plain NES picture at 240, no smoothing, no flicker fix.
  Fixed (s_prepared set again after the check). Pacing: emulation ~10.2 ms median,
  [later] on most frames -> Second core On. MangoHud flat at 240.
- User after the fix: "darn near perfect". Two things:
  1) The 1-1 fence "flashes" when scrolling starts/stops.
  2) The item lanterns shake (like NG2's red power-ups).

### Lanterns: the sprite camera (fixed)
- NG1 places objects (lanterns, Ryu, ...) with a SPRITE camera that is the same
  half-pixel camera rounded the other way: over a run it steps as the scenery did the
  frame before (1,2,1,2 vs 2,1,2,1); from rest it makes the other step (scenery 1 -> 2,
  2 -> 1); stopping, it stops with the scenery (a frame later after a single step).
  Rule checked on every still-lantern frame: right 3,135, wrong 30 (prev-frame camera
  alone: wrong 134). On the NES a still lantern wobbles 1 px against the scenery.
- Glide: per character, score which camera gives a steadier world motion (GlLag);
  lagging characters move by their step on the sprite camera and are drawn the sprite
  camera's lead (s_spr_lead) further. Each tile remembers its answer (s_tile_lag), so a
  lantern scrolling into view starts corrected.
- Ryu and the lanterns share palette 0 and touch: grouped as one character, the lantern
  took Ryu's motion. Now: lagging and non-lagging pieces never group, and item tiles
  $ED-$FB only group with each other.
- tools/glide_harness.c rewritten for NG1 (HUD split at 56): lantern drift vs the
  scenery at 240 over the whole recording: 4,941 px -> 892 px (-82%); pictures moving
  > 0.3 px: 2,615 -> 610.

### Fence flash at scroll starts (fixed)
- ease_band() started a band with no history (stage start, respawn, after a cinema scene)
  TWO steps behind (d0 = -d, then x += d), so the scenery jerked back 2 px before moving.
  Now d0 = 0 (one step behind = where the last picture was). Whole recording: pictures
  moving against the camera 243 -> 157, of which more than 1 px: 41 -> 0.
  (This bug is in ContraRecomp/NG2's extras too.)

## Second play test (2026-11-15): power-ups fixed; starts and Ryu still uneven

User: lanterns fixed, to Act 2, "way easier on the eyes". Still: going from stop to moving
is "too fast / uneven" at the fence; Ryu looks slightly uneven.

### Starts (fixed)
- Simulated ease_band() on a start from rest (scenery 0 -> 2,1,2,1): the picture crept off
  (0.7, 1.0 px/frame), fell behind, then overshot to 1.73 and wobbled 1.6/1.7 for ~10
  frames catching up. NG1's camera goes 0 -> 1.5 px/frame at once.
- Now a start (last step 0, picture caught up) or a new band begins at the last run's
  speed (BandMotion.run; 1.5 the first time): 1.68, 1.32, then 1.56/1.44.
  Whole recording: pictures faster than 1.65 px/frame 10,803 -> 4,306.

### Ryu (fixed)
- His body (tiles $20/$22) stays put on screen in the game; his legs change tiles every 4
  frames. Only 2 of 4-5 pieces matched by tile (under Contra's majority rule), so his step
  was guessed from his average speed (1.33 vs the camera's 1.5): a 0.65 px pop every 4
  frames. Now the follow-by-position path measures each piece's step to its nearest
  piece of last frame and uses the step most pieces agree on (70%).
- Harness (Acts 1-6, palette-0 pieces): uneven pictures 3.3% -> 1.4%, mean step change
  0.033 -> 0.016 px. Lantern drift 777 -> 723 px.

## Third play test: "way better", Ryu great running; starts still a slight flash on fences

- Cause: the game's camera goes 0 -> 1.5 px/frame in one frame; at 240 pictures/s the
  fine 1-px fence patterns show the sudden speed change.
- New menu row Smooth motion -> "Camera easing" ([Game] CameraEasing): 0 Quick (the old
  4-step average, starts at full speed), 1 Gentle (default), 2 Softest. Gentle/Softest:
  the SCENERY follows the moving average of the camera's last 6/8 positions: starts and
  stops ramp over 6/8 frames, a run is exactly 1.5 px/frame (1,2,1,2 averages out), at
  most 4/5.5 px behind (edge crop). Characters keep the quick easing (with Gentle on
  characters too, a fast enemy was drawn 13 px / 2.5 frames behind its real spot).
- A new scroll band starts at rest; an unmatched (just appeared) sprite moves with the
  scenery band under it (+ sprite-camera lead if its tile lags), instead of jumping when
  matched a frame later.
- Harness, Act 1 start at the fence (px/frame): Quick 1.68 1.32 1.68...; Gentle 0.32 0.48
  0.84 1.0 1.32 1.52 1.52; stop: Gentle 1.52 1.16 1.0 0.68 ... Ryu uneven 1.4% -> 1.0%,
  lanterns unchanged (591 px drift). Death/transition frames (scroll 0 for one frame) give
  a few 1-4 px picture jumps in Gentle (13 in the recording): left as is.
