## Things to expect (from the ROM, to confirm in Phase 5)

- HUD split by SPRITE 0 HIT, not a scanline IRQ (MMC1 has no IRQ). The ROM polls
  $2002 bit 6 (wait for clear, then wait for set) at three places:
  bank 4 $8250, bank 7 $F198 and bank 7 $F5A2. Likely gameplay HUD at the TOP
  (like NG2's split at line 48) plus the cinema scenes.
- Only 7 writes to $2005 in the first 128 KB scan: a small scroll routine, easy to
  follow in scroll.log.
- CHR ROM banked 4 KB at a time: cinema scenes may switch CHR mid-frame (NG2's
  cinema scenes did; left them untouched there).
- Flicker: the original game flickers sprites when more than 8 share a line (seen even
  on a CRT). Fixed by "No sprite flicker" (Phase 6 #2). Check whether it rotates or
  reverses OAM (NG2 rotated every frame).
- Uneven scrolling / enemy steps: NG2 had the camera on 1,2,1,2 and soldiers on 0/1 px
  steps. Expect the same family of code here; NG2's eased camera, bands kept 30 frames
  and eased glide are the fixes to start from (Contra's extras include them).
